About this event

Story Times are a great place to meet other families and join our story time community. Together we will practice our ABCs, counting to 10, and try out our animal sounds!  The Story Time is geared towards babies and toddlers of all ages. Through reading, talking, and singing, Story Time helps your little one develop pre-literacy skills.  Questions? Please email Ms. Val at valerie.coughlin@hobokenlibrary.org
