About this event

Join us at the Grand Street Branch on Wednesdays for a half-hour of movement-based learning and interactive play! Open to children of all ages. We'll practice our social skills and gross motor skills through half an hour of singing and dancing along to popular children's songs. Stay and play games, blocks, trains, and puzzles to practice fine motor skills. Board games are available for older children. Questions? Please email grandstreet@hobokenlibrary.org.
