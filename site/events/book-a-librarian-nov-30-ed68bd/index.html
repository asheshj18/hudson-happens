About this event

Do you need help with an online application? Do you want to set up an email account but don’t know how? Do you need assistance with tracing your family tree? Why not try the Bayonne Public Library’s “Book A Librarian” service! Appointments are available on Monday afternoons between 2:30 and 3:30 pm and on Thursday evenings between 5:30 and 7:30 pm. Bayonne patrons may schedule a 45-minute appointment for individualized assistance on a variety of topics. We can help with: ● Online applications ● Research including local history & genealogy ● Using Library resources such as databases,
