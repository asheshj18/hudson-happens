About this event

Homework Help is a peer tutoring program for teens that empowers students by providing academic support through peer collaboration. This program connects students with peer tutors who assist with homework and other academic challenges, creating a nurturing environment where learning is both supportive and engaging. TAB Members can earn volunteer services hours by volunteering as peer tutors!
