About this event

This delightful program invites community members to enjoy complimentary hot chocolate served by our enthusiastic teens, creating an atmosphere filled with good vibes and genuine connections. As you sip on your delicious cup of cocoa, engage in uplifting conversations, share stories, and forge new friendships. Good Vibes & Hot Cocoa is more than just a beverage giveaway, it's a celebration of community spirit, kindness, and the power of youth-led engagement!
