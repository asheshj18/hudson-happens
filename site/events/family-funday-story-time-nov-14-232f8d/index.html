About this event

Story Time will be led by children's book author and chef, Rene Hewitt! This fun and interactive class will whisk your children into a world of culinary creativity.  Children will learn about healthy ingredients, basic cooking techniques, and kitchen safety, all while having a blast and making new friends. Register today to reserve your spot.  Family FUNday is sponsored by the Friends & Foundation of the Hoboken Public Library. About Chef Rene Hewitt: Chef Rene Hewitt is a culinary instructor, children’s book author, and creator of Chef Rene Hewitt’s Sizzlin Kitchen™, his custom ChatGPT AI
