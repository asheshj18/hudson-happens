About this event

Little Spanish Scholars presents a fun and interactive bilingual Spanish Story Time with music & movement. This class is for ages 1 to 5 and will be on Saturdays at 10:30 am in the Children’s Room of the Bayonne Public Library. The class will be on the following dates: January 17th February 28th March 7th April 25th May 16th June 13th September 26th October 24th November 21st December 12th It is perfect for children and their caregivers who want to immerse themselves in the Spanish language through storytelling, song, and dance. Registration is required every session individually.
