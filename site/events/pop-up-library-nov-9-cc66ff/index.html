About this event

Librarians from the Hoboken Public Library are so excited to bring a Pop-Up Library to Brandt Elementary School! During the Pop-Up Library Visit you can: Pick out 1 new book to borrow Return any library items you have at home or at school Questions? Email Ms. Val at valerie.coughlin@hobokenlibrary.org. Make sure to bring your library card to school with you!
