About this event

Join Mayor James Solomon and the City of Jersey City for an unforgettable holiday concert featuring the New Jersey Symphony at the historic Loew's Theatre! This special celebration is free for Jersey City residents, with priority access for seniors and JC students. Special thanks to our generous sponsors for helping make this inaugural holiday concert possible. We'd also like to encourage attendees to bring a new, unwrapped toy in support of the City's Toy Drive.
