About this event

In collaboration with Konnekt Global's Konnekting Generations program, we are offering free technology classes at the Grand Street Branch Library. This 6-week series will focus on smartphones, led by a team of Hoboken High School student volunteers! October 13: Email basics October 27: AI awareness & how to use it November 10: Online safety & scam protection (with Project Senior Care) November 24: Video calling (Zoom, FaceTime, Google Meet) December 8: Online shopping & banking safely December 22: Organizing & backing up photos to the cloud You do not need to register for all classes.
