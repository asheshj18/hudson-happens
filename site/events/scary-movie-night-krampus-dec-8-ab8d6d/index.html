About this event

Join us for a screening of Krampus (2015)! Light refreshments will be served. While the holiday season represents the most magical time of year, ancient European folklore warns of Krampus, a horned beast who punishes naughty children at Christmas. When dysfunctional family squabbling causes Max to lose his festive spirit, it unleashes the wrath of the fearsome demon. As Krampus lays siege to the home, Max's mother (Toni Collette) and father (Adam Scott), sister, and brother band together to stop the evil force and save their Christmas.
