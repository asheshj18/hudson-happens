About this event

Meet us for a story time that focuses on singing, dancing, and play. While we may not be reading books, Baby Music Class still helps promote your little ones early literacy skills with a focus on vocabulary expansion, and memory development, Singing and dancing with the crowd will help our little ones (and adults!) foster community through shared experience.  Registration is not required.
