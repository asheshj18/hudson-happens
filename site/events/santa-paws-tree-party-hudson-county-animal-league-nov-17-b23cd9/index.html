About this event

Join the Hudson County Animal League for a party celebrating their 22nd annual Santa Paws Tree from 7-9 pm in the Lobby. Honor your pet (living or in loving memory) by helping us decorate the 22nd annual “Santa Paws Tree” sponsored by the Hudson County Animal League! Our tree honors all pets - paws, claws, feathers, fur, fins, and scales. A picture or name ornament will be created of your pet for a donation of $10 or more. All donations are tax deductible.
