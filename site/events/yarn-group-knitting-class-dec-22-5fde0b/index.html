About this event

Join Susan Averello at the Bayonne Public Library for a yarn group & knitting class! She has been knitting for over 30 years and she can also crochet. She has worked at Michaels as a knitting teacher and with Crafty Fox Studios until the studio closed. The group will meet on Tuesdays from 7-8:30 pm in the Art & Music Room on the 2nd floor of the Bayonne Public Library. Knitters and crocheters of all experience levels are welcome, including those who would like to learn. No registration is required.
