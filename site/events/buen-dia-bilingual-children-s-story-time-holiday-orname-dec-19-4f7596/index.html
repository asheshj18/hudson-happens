About this event

Celebrate Puerto Rican stories and holiday traditions at ¡Buen Día! Bilingual Children's Story Time, presented by the Boricua Community Building Center in partnership with Bayonne Public Library and Nuyorican Books! Join Puerto Rican children's author Samantha J. Garcia-Sherpa, founder of Nuyorican Books, for an engaging bilingual reading featuring “I Am Boricua”, “ABCs for Baby Boricuas”, and “ABCs for Baby Boricuas: Pueblos”.
