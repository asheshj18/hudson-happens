About this event

Would you like to learn English? Would you like to improve? Learn English to improve your understanding and use of English. You will learn grammar in use, vocabulary, and speaking. Join Bayonne Adult ESL at the Bayonne Public Library in Classroom B in the basement for ESL classes! Classes will run October 12th through December 18th. Morning classes will be held from 10 am to 12 pm on Mondays through Thursdays. Evening classes will be held from 6:30 pm to 8:30 pm on Wednesdays and Thursdays.
