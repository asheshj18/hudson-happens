About this event

Join Bayonne Public Library and Mamas in Bayonne for our November 2026 Bayonne Book Club! All are welcomed and encouraged to attend. The genre / theme of the books chosen will vary. The discussions will be moderated by Bayonne local, Angela van Genderen (@angela_the_ninja). This month’s meeting will be Thursday, November 19th at 7 pm in the Small Programming Room on the 2nd Floor at the Bayonne Public Library.
