About this event

This vibrant club provides a welcoming environment where teens can engage in lively debate games, allowing them to explore diverse topics while having fun. Participants will learn the essentials of formal debating, including its structure, rules, and effective techniques to present compelling arguments. Guided by TAB Member Gabriel, teens will have the opportunity to practice public speaking, develop persuasive strategies, and enhance their ability to think on their feet.
