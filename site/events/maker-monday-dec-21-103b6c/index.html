About this event

Meet us for a weekly kid's craft program! Perfect for young artists and their caregivers who are eager to learn and create in a friendly, supportive environment. Our rotating craft sessions are designed to engage young minds and fine-tune their artistic skills through a variety of themes and materials. All materials will be provided, and no prior experience is necessary. We look forward to crafting with you! Questions? Please email grandstreet@hobokenlibrary.org.
