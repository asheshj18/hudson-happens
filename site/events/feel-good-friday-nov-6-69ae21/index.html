About this event

Every Friday, we encourage children and their caregivers to share in a story at the library. Each child who checks out a book or stays and reads with us can enjoy a treat! Learn about library programs, listen to music, and spend time reading with your child.  Questions? Please email grandstreet@hobokenlibrary.org.
