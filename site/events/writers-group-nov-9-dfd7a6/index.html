About this event

If you are working on your next project—whether it is a novel, short story, memoir or poetry—our Writers Group is a great place to get input and constructive feedback on your work! Our supportive group of fellow writers can help give you insights you need into shaping your work for self or traditional publication. Email hplwriters@gmail.com for more information and to share your work with group. Please email at least a week in advance any writing you would like to share with the group. Please read the other writer's work you are sent and bring at least one thing you enjoyed about their piece
