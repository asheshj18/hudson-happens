About this event

This story time will feature picture books, nursery rhymes, finger plays, and music. Through reading, talking, and singing, Story Time helps your little one develop early literacy skills. Our weekly Story Times are a great place to meet other families and join our story time community. Attending story times regularly helps prep your toddler for the transition to preschool as they develop the necessary listening and social skills they’ll need in the classroom.  Questions? Please email grandstreet@hobokenlibrary.org.
