About this event

The Arts & Crafts program is on Thursdays from 3:15-4 pm. It is for children aged 6-10 years old. Each week features themed crafts and stories. No advanced registration is required. Spots will be filled on a first come, first served basis. We ask that parents of children under 9 years old remain in the room during the program.
