About this event

Join us at the Bayonne Public Library on Monday, November 16th at 7 pm in the Gallery for a special presentation “Is My Moody Teen Okay?” by NJ4S Hudson County Partners in Prevention. This organization works to deliver comprehensive mental health support to K-12 students and families in Hudson County. This presentation will explore teen brain development, hormones, mood changes, and emotional well-being, helping parents better understand and support their teens through adolescence. Both parents and teens are welcome and encouraged to attend. No advanced registration is needed. We hope to see
