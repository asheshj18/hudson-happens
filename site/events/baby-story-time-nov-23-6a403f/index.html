About this event

This story time is geared toward babies and their caregivers and includes board books, finger play rhymes, and lapsit songs! This program is meant for babies ages 0-2 and their caregivers. Strollers are not permitted in the building! To ensure everyone has a wonderful experience, please arrive early as spots are limited. Our Baby Story Time session will kick off promptly at 11:45pm.
