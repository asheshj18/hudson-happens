About this event

If you are working on your next project—whether it is a novel, short story, memoir or poetry—our Writers Group is a great place to get input and constructive feedback on your work! Our supportive group of fellow writers can help give you insights you need into shaping your work for self or traditional publication. The group is currently meeting via Zoom. Email hplwriters@gmail.com for more information, to share your work with the group and to be emailed the Zoom invite.
