About this event

Join Mr. Kieran for a diverse range of activities that cater to every taste: unleash your inner artist with craft sessions, dive into immersive gaming experiences, embark on epic adventures with Dungeons & Dragons, and get the support you need with homework help. The Teen Zone is a welcoming space where you can make new friends, express yourself, and discover new hobbies.
