About this event

Whether as a defense against predators, a magical source of immortality, a lethal weapon, or a lifesaving medical treatment, the story of poison is full of surprising turns. This premium exhibition organized by the American Museum of Natural History explores the many facets of poison—its roles in nature, history, myth, health, and forensic science—and shows the immense power of transformation that can be contained in a tiny drop.
