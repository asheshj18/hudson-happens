About this event

Autism Conference 2026 – The World Congress on Autism and Neurodevelopmental Disorders will take place on November 19–20, 2026, in London, UK, with virtual participation available. The conference brings together researchers, clinicians, psychologists, therapists, educators, healthcare professionals, and autism specialists to explore the latest developments in autism research, diagnosis, early intervention, neurobiology, behavioral approaches, inclusive education, sensory processing, co-occurring conditions, and lifelong support.
