About this event

Through one longer story and lots of yoga poses our Yoga Story Time focuses on developing early literacy skills through reading and play.  Movement in story time increases your little ones engagement, attention, and comprehension. Acting as animals and objects from our stories help our young readers begin to understand narrative structure. To ensure everyone has a wonderful experience, please arrive early as spots are limited. Our Story Time session will kick off promptly at 11:45pm.
