About this event

Explore music and language with Bilingual Birdies! Each class is taught by a live musician who teaches new vocabulary through songs, movement, dance, and puppetry. Families get to experience a joyful bilingual adventure in English and Spanish. Join us as we make learning a new language fun and easy! This program is for ages 18 months - 5 and their caregivers. First-come, first served. Please arrive on time as we may not be able to accommodate latecomers.
