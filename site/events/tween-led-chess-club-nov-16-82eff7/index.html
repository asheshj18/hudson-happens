About this event

Come and join our chess club at the Grand Street Branch Library! Guided by Oli, tweens and teens will have the opportunity to play chess together in a supportive environment. Beginner and intermediate players are welcome. Chess Club is for tweens and teens ages 10-15. Chess boards are provided but feel free to bring your own. This walk-in program takes place every other Monday in the Study Room of the Grand Street Branch Library.
