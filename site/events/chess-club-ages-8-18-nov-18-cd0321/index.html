About this event

Join us at Bayonne Public Library for a Chess Club with Phil Rosado! Phil is the author of “Mind Matter: Welcome to Chess!” This weekly class for ages 8-18 meets from 6-7 pm in the Children’s Room. Attendees will learn how to play chess and meet other novices. Advanced registration is required for every session that you plan to attend. Walk-ins will be included if space permits.
