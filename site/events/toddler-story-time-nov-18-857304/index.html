About this event

Through reading, talking, singing, and dancing, Toddler Story Time helps your little one develop their early literacy skills with a focus on phonological awareness, vocabulary expansion, and print awareness. Attending story times regularly helps prep your toddler for the transition to preschool as they develop the necessary listening and social skills they’ll need in the classroom. This program is geared for children 18 months to 3 years old. Questions? Please email grandstreet@hobokenlibrary.org.
